Why this hub is structured this way

The goal is not to mass-produce thin pages. It is to keep a small group of strong landing pages indexed, while still letting other model pages exist and mature over time.

Index selectively

Only a handful of pages are treated as long-term organic assets right now.

Review continuously

Each model page carries review metadata so stale pages can be revised, noindexed, or retired before quality drifts.

Route with intent

Users can still compare and click through the full model set without forcing every page into search results immediately.
